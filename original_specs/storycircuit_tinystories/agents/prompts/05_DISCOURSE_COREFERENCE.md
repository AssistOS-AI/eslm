# Discourse and Coreference Prompt

Implement mention detection, entity persistence, antecedent candidate generation, quotation scope, recency, grammatical role, number/gender compatibility, and calibrated abstention. Preserve alternatives where evidence is insufficient. Never resolve a pronoun merely to make a later answer convenient. Evaluate entity tracking under distractors, reordered mentions, repeated names, dialogue, lexical substitutions, and longer stories. Every resolution must expose its evidence and competing candidates.
