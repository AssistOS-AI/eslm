# Narrative Planner and Surface Realizer Prompt

Induce and execute narrative plans that maintain character identity, state consistency, causal progression, and requested constraints. Keep plan generation separate from surface realization. Realize through weighted constructions with open vocabulary support; verify by re-parsing, simulation, and constraint checking. Report failure rather than repeatedly patching text invisibly. Evaluate coherence, ending plausibility, diversity, repetition, constraint satisfaction, and likelihood under independent baselines.
