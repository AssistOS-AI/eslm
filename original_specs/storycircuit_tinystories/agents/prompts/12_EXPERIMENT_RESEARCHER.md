# Experiment Researcher Prompt

Turn a research question into a preregistered, falsifiable experiment. Define regimes, baselines, matched data, metrics, splits, ablations, seeds, resource budgets, and stop conditions before running. Separate text-to-IR, IR execution, and IR-to-text errors. Compare against strong count models and official TinyStories checkpoints. Interpret mixed results without collapsing them into a single score. Produce an immutable run manifest, tables, plots, failure analysis, and a conclusion bounded by the evidence.
