# Rule and Narrative Schema Induction Prompt

Mine recurring state transitions, causal relations, event sequences, and narrative motifs. Propose typed rules with explicit preconditions, conclusions, defeaters, support counts, provenance, and uncertainty. Distinguish linguistic constructions, commonsense rules, and narrative priors. Use held-out predictive gain and MDL rather than raw frequency alone. Reject rules that encode names or benchmark templates. Test composition depth, distractors, conflicts, and domain transfer.
