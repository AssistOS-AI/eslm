# References and Primary Resources

The package uses short citation keys in technical documents.

- **[EldanLi2023]** Ronen Eldan and Yuanzhi Li. *TinyStories: How Small Can Language Models Be and Still Speak Coherent English?* arXiv:2305.07759. https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07759
- **[TinyStoriesData]** Official TinyStories dataset, prompts, metadata, and model links. https://huggingface.co/datasets/roneneldan/TinyStories
- **[TinyStories1M]** Official TinyStories-1M checkpoint. https://huggingface.co/roneneldan/TinyStories-1M
- **[BabyLM2025]** BabyLM official evaluation pipeline. https://github.com/babylm/evaluation-pipeline-2025
- **[BLiMP]** Alex Warstadt et al. *BLiMP: The Benchmark of Linguistic Minimal Pairs for English.* TACL 2020. https://aclanthology.org/2020.tacl-1.25/
- **[EWoK]** Anna A. Ivanova et al. *Elements of World Knowledge.* arXiv:2405.09605. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.09605
- **[bAbI]** Jason Weston et al. *Towards AI-Complete Question Answering: A Set of Prerequisite Toy Tasks.* arXiv:1502.05698. https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.05698
- **[bAbICode]** Official bAbI task generator. https://github.com/facebookarchive/bAbI-tasks
- **[CLUTRR]** Koustuv Sinha et al. *CLUTRR: A Diagnostic Benchmark for Inductive Reasoning from Text.* arXiv:1908.06177. https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.06177
- **[CLUTRRCode]** Official CLUTRR implementation. https://github.com/facebookresearch/clutrr
- **[StoryCloze]** Nasrin Mostafazadeh et al. *A Corpus and Evaluation Framework for Deeper Understanding of Commonsense Stories.* arXiv:1604.01696. https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.01696
- **[NarrativeQA]** Tomáš Kočiský et al. *The NarrativeQA Reading Comprehension Challenge.* arXiv:1712.07040. https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.07040
- **[EntityTracking]** Najoung Kim and Sebastian Schuster. *Entity Tracking in Language Models.* ACL 2023. https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.213/
- **[TextWorld]** Marc-Alexandre Côté et al. *TextWorld: A Learning Environment for Text-based Games.* arXiv:1806.11532. https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.11532
- **[ART]** Chandra Bhagavatula et al. *Abductive Commonsense Reasoning.* arXiv:1908.05739. https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.05739
- **[AMR]** Laura Banarescu et al. *Abstract Meaning Representation for Sembanking.* ACL 2013. https://aclanthology.org/W13-2322/
- **[TimeML]** James Pustejovsky et al. *TimeML: Robust Specification of Event and Temporal Expressions in Text.* https://www.timeml.org/publications/timeMLdocs/annguide_1.2.1.pdf
- **[ProbLog]** Luc De Raedt, Angelika Kimmig, and Hannu Toivonen. *ProbLog: A Probabilistic Prolog and Its Application in Link Discovery.* IJCAI 2007.
- **[EventCalculus]** Robert Kowalski and Marek Sergot. *A Logic-based Calculus of Events.* New Generation Computing, 1986.
- **[STRIPS]** Richard Fikes and Nils Nilsson. *STRIPS: A New Approach to the Application of Theorem Proving to Problem Solving.* Artificial Intelligence, 1971.
- **[MDL]** Jorma Rissanen. *Modeling by Shortest Data Description.* Automatica, 1978.
- **[Sequitur]** Craig Nevill-Manning and Ian Witten. *Identifying Hierarchical Structure in Sequences: A Linear-Time Algorithm.* JAIR, 1997.
