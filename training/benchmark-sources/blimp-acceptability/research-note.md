# BLiMP feature-grammar development cycle

The official BLiMP archive is pinned to commit `3e56b06fcabca9b30822fc66435fca6b1aa40bb1`, is distributed under CC BY 4.0, and has the verified archive SHA-256 `cbada5cc59b41798f0f0a6b2525166c7a1d82c4a40ed726c78810b898e1979f6`. The adapter validates all 67 JSONL files, all allowlisted fields, all 1,000 pairs per paradigm, unique pair identifiers, and the correspondence between each file name and its `UID` metadata.

The deterministic partition ranks every pair within its paradigm by `sha256(seed + NUL + paradigm + NUL + caseId)`, using seed `eslm-blimp-development-fresh-v1`. Ranks 0–799 form the development-visible pool; ranks 800–999 form the fresh pool. The development membership digest is `ccbb95847a8a4b8499963420fad3a6feb8cc58752ff098b56dc9559bedeae439`; the fresh membership digest is `65930206f3cfc9f01582a1aadc53d220ef7e7b98f7e237e67aeef688e0d4851d`. No fresh sentence or fresh preference label was inspected before candidate freeze.

The pre-change scalar grammar scorer passed 6,294 of 53,600 development pairs, tied 38,168 pairs, and therefore had 11.742537313432835% exact preference accuracy. The frozen feature candidate passes 47,389 of 53,600 pairs, ties 4,594, reverses 1,617, and has 88.41231343283582% exact preference accuracy.

The development-visible feature builder converts construction evidence into declarative lexical and grammatical metadata. Its output contains no case identifier, source sentence, expected label, or paradigm key. The generic core receives only normalized surfaces and semantic or grammatical features: category, number, agreement, animacy, gender, form, valency, polarity, quantifier strength, clause boundary, gap licensing, expletive selection, infinitive dependency, and ellipsis category. Core source is statically audited against benchmark names and inspected answer vocabulary. Nonce, renamed, pair-reordered, and contrastive tests exercise the mechanism without source vocabulary.

The remaining development errors are structural rather than access failures. The largest unresolved clusters are long-distance subject-gap attachment (298/800), adjunct-island accessibility (475/800), transitive animacy selection (537/800), N-bar ellipsis (575/800 and 561/800), adjective-mediated determiner agreement (586–688/800), relative-clause distractor agreement (591/800), inchoative/optional valency (581/800), irregular verbal participle ambiguity (574/800), local binding domains (618–688/800), sentential-subject islands (632/800), and wh-island attachment (630/800). Reaching 100% would require a fuller phrase-structure representation, explicit clause attachment, lexical ambiguity resolution, and coordination-aware gap threading. Adding paradigm-specific branches or memorized surfaces would violate the core-generalization gate and was rejected.

After the behavior and profile were frozen, the fresh partition was scored once without printing or inspecting any sentence or individual result. It passed 11,767 of 13,400 pairs, tied 1,227, reversed 406, and achieved 87.81343283582089% exact preference accuracy. The development-to-fresh difference is 0.59888059701493 percentage points, consistent with the candidate encoding reusable grammatical features rather than development pair identities.
