Reasoning methods: semantics, algorithms, witnesses, and acceptance boundaries

ESLM reserves “reasoning” for registered mathematical operations over validated representations. Each method descriptor states the accepted types, preconditions, soundness and completeness boundary, uncertainty semantics, resource model, and witness. The executor satisfies that contract under nonce renaming, changed ordering, counterexamples, and explicit bounds.

The planner chooses by semantic type and declared capability. The method receives typed evidence rather than dataset identity and returns an independently checkable derivation, model, path, transition trace, feature account, or explicit gap.

The method descriptor is a falsifiable claim

src/reasoning/capability-registry.mjs requires a method identifier, input and output types, preconditions, soundness, completeness, and proof kind. Cost and uncertainty fields add planning and interpretation information. A descriptor does not prove the implementation correct. The corresponding tests must show that the output witness reconstructs the computation, that unrelated ordering does not change the answer, that meaning-changing variants do change it, and that crossing a declared bound returns RESOURCE_LIMIT rather than a guessed answer.

Soundness and completeness are always qualified. Safe Horn closure is sound for accepted positive range-restricted rules. Finite entailment is complete for its bounded propositional atom set. Scalable Boolean entailment remains complete for finite propositional semantics only while its explicit search bounds are not reached; query-directed reduction does not make it a first-order prover. Narrative ranking is a deterministic implementation of a defeasible scoring policy; it is neither deductively sound nor a calibrated probability of the real world.

Five technical chapters

Deduction and finite models derives the least-fixed-point and finite-model algorithms, their complexity, and their proof obligations. Categorical logic defines A/E/I/O truth conditions, the traditional square, valid transformations, finite-population syllogistic entailment, and the subalternation ambiguity. Defaults, induction, and abduction explains why these results are non-monotonic or hypothetical and how the implementation prevents them from masquerading as strict facts. State, time, and relation composition describes transition systems, predecessor semantics, dynamic composition, breadth-first path states, and the current boundary with interval reasoning. Narrative and compatibility reasoning explains the event-frame approximation, feature equations, provider evidence, margins, and why the method is defeasible.

Planner integration and outcome interpretation

The current planner is narrower than the full AND/OR target. It normally receives one semantic goal, selects a deterministic candidate from declared capabilities, and emits an OBSERVE → DERIVE → VERIFY → CONSTRUCT plan. A method's UNKNOWN means that its declared evidence scope was exhausted without a result. NO_APPLICABLE_METHOD means the required transformation is absent. RESOURCE_LIMIT means the method or retrieval frontier was not exhausted. AMBIGUOUS and UNDERDETERMINED preserve several admissible semantic results rather than selecting the first.

Theory and implementation authority

The normative method semantics are in DS015. Task frames, planning, traces, and result schemas are in DS008. The source modules and tests remain the executable evidence. External papers explain the formal families but do not broaden ESLM's actual claims.
