Trusted strategy checkpoint

The strategy architecture now has a concrete coordinated slice. All 24 deterministic language-approximation families execute through the sealed typed registry and synchronous stage coordinator, with canonical order, advance work allocation, bounded result validation, correlation groups, and an execution receipt. The direct parser remains the mandatory language authority gate, and the DS022 proposal lattice remains the final interpretation arbiter.

The generated development suite exposed cross-domain routing failures that are now handled generically. Explicit artifact requests are planned query-locally even when the direct parser could mistake their sentence-like material for assertions. Local approximation inspects UNPARSED and UNKNOWN, and it can challenge direct SOLVED or PARTIAL when a visible structural candidate has different parse-only Semantic IR. Equal IR preserves direct success, every changed interpretation is query-local, a normal knowledge gap remains unchanged without an accepted candidate, and only a final UNPARSED may reach the opt-in Language Agent.

Exact --strategy-select and interactive /strategy allowlists also gate request planning, query focus, relevance features, reasoning methods, and result construction inside their current owner modules. Those entries are accurately labeled instrumented-local. Named --strategy-preset and /strategies PRESET values filter only the inventory view. Retrieval-frontier execution, method planning, failure grounding, independent verification, and compiler acquisition remain non-selectable or planned until their real executors cross the shared coordinator boundary. The trusted strategy architecture page shows the dataflow, exact CLI syntax, receipt walkthrough, and completion gates.

Unanswered questions now preserve useful KB context

If the runtime cannot support an answer, it does not convert a nearby fact into one. For eligible UNKNOWN and UNPARSED results, it may attach a separate, bounded grounding bundle containing related entries, contributor KB versions, search receipts, truncation, and completeness. The bundle says answerSupported: false. It can help a person reformulate the question or ground a downstream LLM, while the primary answer and provenance remain unchanged.

Grounding uses role-weighted focus over the visible request: accepted entities and predicates, content phrases, nouns, and verbs outrank grammatical operators and request scaffolding. It queries exact bounded postings and retains the session state produced earlier in the same episode. It does not run after RESOURCE_LIMIT, and incomplete provider searches cannot claim that no related evidence exists. See grounded failure recovery for examples and heuristic language approximation for query focus and work policies.

Evidence labels matter

The local evaluate and benchmark:authored scripts execute five authored fixture cases apiece. The separate benchmark:generated route executes 1,200 deterministic nonce and domain instantiations of 43 reviewed heuristic shapes across 18 domains and reports collective failures by oracle level, structure, technique, target family, complexity, route, status, confidence, and resource outcome. Its six contract levels distinguish answer execution, Semantic IR, query-local decomposition, request execution, safe abstention, and proposal-only preservation; a proposal-only pass with final UNPARSED is not executable interpretation coverage. The default npm run benchmark runs both and keeps their artifacts and denominators separate. All three internal reports carry benchmarkComparable: false.

The generated suite is development-visible and project-owned. It provides much broader evidence than one motivating sentence and makes repeated failure clusters actionable, while public rows remain the evidence for externally defined raw-language, source-template, structured-task, source-annotation, or solver-conformance tracks. Those regimes validate different layers and must not share an unlabeled headline score. Current generated outcomes are available through the generated heuristic report; this page does not duplicate its mutable counts.

A report can also be assembled today from an older frozen receipt. The report timestamp is assembly time; it is not the execution time of every row. Receipt audits distinguish current, historical-stale, historical-unrecoverable, invalid, and unavailable evidence. Forced-choice abstentions stay in the full denominator: a method that attempts no cases has 0% end-to-end accuracy, 0% attempt coverage, and no selective accuracy. The full dashboard and row-level diagnoses live only on the evaluation page.

A current row must also bind its content-addressed strategy catalog, exact allowlists or adapter-local state, requested and effective work profile, configured arbiters, and aggregate coordinated stage-receipt digests. Catalog inclusion and arbiter configuration are not execution. An older row without this bounded strategy snapshot remains historical or reporting-incomplete until a clean rerun records it.

Next release gates

  1. Evidence integrity: freeze a clean code and dependency identity, rerun feasible development tracks in isolated processes, measure real peak memory and elapsed time, and mark stale or unrecoverable receipts as historical.
  2. Generalization: replace row-hash “fresh” splits with generator-, template-, lexicon-, world-, proof-graph-, and source-grouped splits. Keep raw language separate from formulas and task adapters.
  3. Grounded failure quality: freeze independently authored answerable, partial, unanswerable, ambiguous, conflicting, typo, paraphrase, multi-KB, and distractor queries. Score answer support, abstention, retrieval recall and precision, citation validity, contradiction, unsupported claims, completeness, latency, and resource use separately.
  4. Production controls: add enforced process-level resource boundaries where promised, expand exact document indexes behind DS018–DS021 gates, and require replayable receipts for release claims.
  5. Strategy completion: migrate the remaining selectable local owners through the common coordinator, add the separate compiler registry and independent verifier stage, freeze exact strategy configurations in benchmark receipts, and rerun affected rows under those identities.

The normative acceptance sequence is DS011. Evaluation and claim rules are in DS010. Source, routing, and cache gates are in DS016 through DS021.
