ESLM Documentation / Real knowledge sources

From public knowledge graphs to executable modules

Benchmark stories can reveal whether a rule engine works, but they cannot supply the breadth required for a useful assistant. ESLM therefore separates evaluation datasets from knowledge corpora. A knowledge corpus contributes persistent language, facts, relations, defaults, or event schemas. An evaluation suite contributes questions and oracles and remains isolated unless an experiment explicitly declares source exposure.

The first build: Open English WordNet 2025

Open English WordNet 2025 is the best first source because it is current, moderate in size, explicitly licensed, and structurally close to the missing linguistic layer. Its concepts are senses rather than untyped strings. The word “bank” can therefore refer to a financial institution or the side of a river without merging both into one entity. Synsets group compatible lemmas, definitions explain each sense, and typed links connect senses through hypernymy, meronymy, antonymy, similarity, entailment, and causation.

The first diagnostic probe has now scanned all 73 JSON members in the 9,986,555-byte archive. It found 107,519 synsets, 185,129 distinct sense IDs, 128,009 lexical-entry keys, 127,311 normalized synset members, 107,524 definitions, and 49,596 examples. Of the normalized members, 25,805 point to more than one synset: “break” alone has 75 senses in the source. The machine-readable probe report contains the relation, field-shape, ambiguity, reference-integrity, and per-stage performance inventories.

The probe exposed representation details that would have been lost in the v1 model. Adjective satellites use the separate s code; lexical entries can use homograph keys such as n-1 and n-2; sense records contain lexical relations distinct from synset relations; and Wikidata values are external identifiers rather than synset targets. Definitions and examples are text, not automatic universal rules. The archive has no hypernym cycle after topological reduction and no unresolved internal synset target in the fields classified as synset relations, but this structural integrity does not make every relation safe for deduction.

The future adapter will preserve the frozen 2025 release and attribution, then create two profiles. core-senses will contain a reproducibly selected lexical core for rapid iteration; full-lexicon will compile the complete release. Full compilation remains blocked until aliases become lemma-to-sense candidate postings, dictionaries and relation shards are implemented, and queries can load only relevant shards.

Taxonomic closure is deductive only after a sense has been resolved: if the selected poodle sense is a dog sense and that dog sense is an animal sense, the executor may conclude that the poodle sense is an animal. Meronymy does not receive blanket transitivity, antonymy does not turn absence into negation, and a textual gloss remains evidence text rather than executable code.

The second build: ATOMIC event knowledge

ATOMIC 2020 contains approximately 1.33 million tuples about intentions, prerequisites, effects, reactions, wants, causes, and event order. Its subjects and objects are often open phrases involving placeholders such as PersonX. These records cannot be imported as unconditional world facts. They require event templates, participant binding, modality, direction, and confidence.

The independently loadable social-core KB will cover actor intentions, prerequisites, effects, reactions, and wants. event-causality will add causes, event order, subevents, and obstacles. For example, an xIntent continuation is a possible intention of the actor, while an xEffect continuation is a typical effect on that actor. These become ranked hypotheses that can answer “why might” and “what could happen”, never categorical statements that an event always occurred.

The third build: filtered English ConceptNet

ConceptNet supplies ordinary relations missing from a lexical taxonomy: a knife is used for cutting, ice has the property cold, a bird may be capable of flying, and food can be found at a restaurant. The bulk 5.7 assertion archive is about 498 MB compressed and multilingual. ESLM will retain only records whose usable endpoints are English, then apply a documented relation allowlist and source-quality policy.

Its independently selectable KBs are english-core, english-physical, and english-everyday-actions. IsA may participate in bounded taxonomic closure. Purpose, location, material, and part relations remain typed retrieval. Capabilities and properties become defeasible defaults only under explicit exception handling. Causes support hypotheses, not certainty. Each assertion retains weight, contributor/source provenance, license, polarity, and overlap with WordNet or DBpedia.

The fourth build: GeoNames geography

GeoNames is a bounded, purpose-specific route to useful geography before Wikidata. The first countries-and-capitals KB will contain countries, capitals, alternate names, feature types, coordinates, and administrative containment. A later global-places profile may extend this coverage only after the streaming and spatial indexes remain within budget.

GeoNames records do not justify arbitrary geographic inference. Administrative containment, named-place identity, coordinates, feature class, and alternate names remain separate fields. Spatial composition states its relation type and coordinate assumptions, and disputed or approximate locations retain source and precision.

Wikidata is future and thematic only

Wikidata remains valuable for named entities, dates, organizations, scientific objects, and cross-source identifiers, but the full truthy dump is more than 43 GB compressed and omits statement qualifiers and references. It is removed from the near-term build queue.

If introduced later, it will only produce bounded dated KBs such as science-core, geography-crosscheck, or history-core. Each pack starts from reviewed domain seeds and property allowlists, retrieves complete revisioned entities where qualifiers matter, and records traversal boundaries. There will be no generic “load Wikidata” command and no full-dump KB.

What the coding agent does at this scale

A coding agent does not manually rewrite millions of rows as JavaScript. It receives source documentation, a frozen train-only sample, relation inventories, frequency summaries, conflicts, and counterexamples. It proposes the mapping from source semantics to ESLM types, selects safe inference rules, designs sharding and indexes, and generates or revises the Node adapter and model templates. Deterministic Node code then streams the entire artifact and emits reproducible modules.

Module and inference contract

Every built profile will have a manifest, interned dictionaries, entity or sense tables, relation-specific fact shards, source metadata, alias indexes, predicate postings, and a conflict index. Rules remain separate from source facts. Query execution loads only named profiles and activates only predicates relevant to the compiled question.

The experiment stops or revises its representation when module growth follows individual records rather than relation families, when source provenance cannot reconstruct answers, when selective loading offers no benefit, when false inheritance remains common after exception handling, or when source-derived evaluations cannot be separated from independent tests.

Inspecting status from the CLI

node src/cli.mjs corpus catalog
node src/cli.mjs corpus status
node src/cli.mjs corpus status --corpus oewn-2025,atomic-2020
node src/cli.mjs corpus probe \
  --corpus oewn-2025 \
  --archive /path/to/english-wordnet-2025-json.zip

For WordNet, status now reports probeComplete: true and names the sense-aware shard prototype as the next artifact. It still reports sourceCached: false, prepared: false, and generatedModel: false: the diagnostic download was temporary and no result is being misreported as a trained KB. corpus probe accepts only the implemented WordNet probe and a local archive path; it writes the training report and its published documentation copy but generates no model. Fetch, prepare, and build commands will be exposed source by source only after the corresponding adapter validates the official artifact.
