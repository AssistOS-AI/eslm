Research horizons grounded in executable architecture

ESLM has a strong deterministic runway for language recovery, retrieval, reasoning, provenance, and honest inability. The next research programs extend that runway through explicit representations, competing techniques, protected experiments, and promotion gates. Difficult problems are named here so they can be studied over successive releases instead of disappearing inside a generic promise of greater intelligence.

The executable baseline supplies measurements and failure receipts. Each research track compares explicit alternatives, then tests them on grouped development and protected pools. Only a deterministic, bounded, generic, and falsifiably safer mechanism crosses the promotion gate. Rejected hypotheses remain useful evidence rather than hidden implementation history.

A shared experimental control plane

DS027 turns these programs into independently replaceable trusted strategies rather than additions to one opaque procedure. A language proposal, retrieval score, method candidate, proof verifier, or document extractor declares its typed stage, preconditions, confidence kind, witness, finite resource dimensions, and failure classes. Exact allowlists select statically registered identities; the work policy and configured arbiter identity are frozen separately. Per-strategy ablations can then ask which technique was applicable, how much work it consumed, what it supported or opposed, and whether its incremental benefit survives grouped and protected evidence.

The control plane also prevents category errors. Several correlated spelling rules cannot manufacture independent confidence; a high retrieval score cannot authorize an answer; an extraction vote cannot promote a source claim; and a failing verifier cannot be outvoted by fluent construction. The registry, inventory, and stage runner already coordinate the language-family slice. The remaining runtime owners and the compiler pipeline migrate through explicit acceptance gates. See Processing-node strategies for the current-versus-target ledger.

The first real coordinated slice is already present: all 24 local language families execute through the sealed registry and stage runner. Exact allowlists also gate request, focus, relevance, reasoning, and construction inside their instrumented-local owners. Named presets remain inventory views only. Ordinary method planning and witness verification now expose bounded local-node receipts; retrieval, method planning, failure grounding, and verification remain non-selectable until their shared-coordinator receipts meet the same standard. Compiler acquisition remains planned.

Dataset-guided processing-graph discovery

DS028 adds an explicit research route for finding missing node types, authority gates, strategies, edges, and protocol fields in rights-cleared public task evidence. Source bytes, research projections, benchmark pools, KB extraction, and generic-core proposals occupy separate authority lanes. Training-visible trajectories become inert typed episodes; upstream actions, loaders, rewards, model rationales, and expected test answers never become runtime control or truth.

Task-frame induction, trajectory motifs, partial-order mining, phase changes, earliest-error analysis, typed preference disagreement, counterfactuals, metamorphic controls, and cross-source recurrence produce competing hypotheses. A coordination node may rank comparable support, but manual contract, security, proof, safety, contamination, and promotion gates remain non-voting. Statistical symbolic relevance

Fast counting can materially improve symbolic retrieval when the count has a declared population and remains subordinate to meaning. The current estimator combines five inspectable sources of evidence: exact phrase or term coverage, co-occurrence of several focus terms, coverage of distinct entity/predicate/object roles, a typed answer-bridge match, and a capped logarithmic vote from occurrence information available in the active KB posting. That occurrence vote has useful priority in close cases, but it is deliberately non-absolute: a ubiquitous term cannot outrank an exact semantic frame by volume alone.

Typed nouns, predicates, entities, objects, and exact phrases form the query focus; grammatical scaffolding does not consume that budget. Exact indexes produce a bounded candidate frontier. Frequency, multi-token co-occurrence, role coverage, and typed answer-bridge signals vote on candidates inside that frontier. The current release stops at the inspectable estimate. The research program adds isolated reasoning probes for top candidates and conflict-aware aggregation into corroborating, contradictory, sense-specific, and subgoal-specific clusters. A ranking score never becomes proof.

The research program compares transparent alternatives such as BM25-style symbolic records, relation-conditioned co-occurrence, deterministic graph spreading, diversity-aware selection, and frozen calibrated linear ensembles. Each alternative must expose feature contributions and retain package, context, trust, conflict, and epistemic status. A bounded reasoning probe may test whether a candidate closes an unresolved atom, but only a registered proof can support the primary answer. Aggregation preserves disagreement instead of manufacturing consensus from several lexically similar records.

Complex language as typed competing analyses

Complex sentences are modeled as competing clause graphs rather than one attractive rewrite. Coordination, ellipsis, relative clauses, apposition, conditionals, temporal and causal relations, passive alternations, references, and embedded questions each need their own scope contract and interaction tests. Negation, quantifiers, conjunction, disjunction, direction, time, and modality must exist as structure; they cannot be hidden inside an opaque class or entity identifier.

The most promising checks combine anchor conservation, typed graph comparison, and bounded countermodel search. Countermodels can disprove equivalence quickly. When no countermodel is found, the transformation still retains its calibrated uncertainty. Ambiguity is a useful terminal interpretation result that asks a precise clarification question; it is not a reason to ask an external agent to choose invisibly.

Multi-obligation planning and verified synthesis

Large requests become ordered obligations with source spans, topics, evidence requirements, negative constraints, dependencies, intermediate outputs, and completion states. “Summarize, compare, then outline” therefore creates three operation-specific selection and construction paths followed by one aggregation step. Confidence ranks alternatives inside an obligation; it does not reorder the user's discourse or erase a secondary task.

Richer generation requires a claim ledger. Every factual sentence records its semantic proposition, supporting records or proof, epistemic strength, output span, realization steps, and verification status. Unsupported sentences are removed, weakened into explicit hypotheses, or listed as gaps. This design opens a path from today's cited extractive drafts to fluent reports and essays while preserving the ability to inspect every factual claim.

Compiler strategies for heterogeneous knowledge

A manual, an API reference, an ontology, a dictionary, an event graph, and a table expose different evidence structures. Treating them as one text-extraction problem loses hierarchy, qualifiers, identity, or cell semantics. The compiler research pipeline therefore separates decode, segmentation, extraction, identity resolution, record standardization, validation, and package compilation. A source-family strategy competes only at its typed stage and preserves exact frozen-source spans, media assumptions, losses, uncertainty, and coverage gaps.

Alternative extractors may agree on a canonical candidate, disagree, or preserve different contexts. Their votes prioritize review; they cannot certify source truth or promote a record. Every route still converges on DS005 schema validation, reference closure, safe rules, provenance, contradiction review, and deterministic DS019 package bytes. Research compares source-span fidelity, qualifier preservation, accepted/rejected/gap coverage, human review work, compiler resources, and canonical-to-compiled equivalence. The separate compiler registry is a planned DS027 surface, so current source scripts and training skills remain the executable baseline.

Multiple reasoning methods and independent verification

The runtime already exposes finite methods for retrieval, Horn deduction, induction, abduction, state transitions, relation composition, spatial constraints, Boolean entailment, countermodels, categorical logic, episodic state, and continuation ranking. Exact allowlists can select the runtime-policy-aware reasoning identities. The adapter-local finite-entailment and preferred-entailment paths remain cataloged as planned until those adapters honor the same gate. The research problem is to coordinate several applicable methods fairly, preserve their distinct soundness and completeness contracts, and verify witnesses independently before construction.

One verified strict proof can authorize a strict claim; several agreeing strict proofs merge provenance and witnesses rather than confidence. A defeasible result remains defeasible even when popular. Strict disagreement is a conflict, and an exhausted or incomplete search cannot vote for falsehood. The ordinary path now has a tested typed runtime.result.verify boundary for retrieval, Horn, induction, abduction, and temporal witnesses. Research therefore measures applicability, method-specific work, proof validity, verifier independence, agreement and conflict, completeness-sensitive absence, and leave-one-method-out behavior while extending that local gate into a selectable multi-method stage.

Realistic end-to-end evidence

The product research suite will contain answerable, partial, unanswerable, ambiguous, conflicting, misspelled, paraphrased, likely-non-English local rejection, separately attributed external translation proposals, multi-KB, wrong-KB-distractor, resource-limited, and multi-request cases. It scores interpretation, retrieval, reasoning, primary answer, abstention, grounded inability, planning, synthesis, citations, and resources separately. This prevents a solver score, lexical match, or helpful downstream formulation from being presented as full end-to-end accuracy.

Splits are grouped by source document, topic, paraphrase family, typo process, predicate vocabulary, proof graph, request pattern, and output schema. A protected pool is opened only after code, packages, policy, scorer, and dependencies are frozen. Opened pools remain historical checkpoints; future progress uses a genuinely new pool.

How the research program learns

Runtime execution never rewrites its own heuristics. Failures are clustered offline by semantic construction, retrieval feature, planning obligation, or proof gap. A proposed improvement includes generic typed behavior, renamed examples, nonce values, changed ordering, meaning-changing controls, a resource bound, and an ablation. It advances only when it improves the intended layer on grouped evidence without weakening inability, provenance, or unsupported-claim gates.

This process supports years of cumulative research because each experiment leaves behind reusable artifacts: frozen judgments, typed failure clusters, feature receipts, rejected alternatives, protected pools, and promotion evidence. The architecture grows through reviewed capabilities instead of a sequence of example-specific patches.
