Discovering better processing graphs from rights-cleared task evidence

ESLM uses a guarded research lane to turn rights-cleared, training-visible task evidence into falsifiable processing-graph hypotheses. Sources are frozen, projected, analyzed, and consolidated through separate contracts. Datasets provide evidence for architecture review; they never become executable control, hidden benchmark knowledge, runtime facts, proof, or answer authority.

Current foundation: the HelpSteer2 + GSM8K pilot has a complete plan-v2, analysis-v6, human review, and cycle-v3 chain. OASST1 is frozen with separate official training and validation membership; 16 validated structural shards admit 2,220 training trees and zero validation trees. The approved combined plan admits 19,854 training-visible episodes and independently bounds analysis selection. Current source-local and combined execution state, work, omissions, and receipt identities come from the validated research status artifact. Historical or superseded receipts remain visible but never impersonate the current chain. No research artifact has changed the 58-node catalog or runtime behavior.

RL and task-feedback sources contribute preferences, quality axes, typed actions, state changes, retries, and outcomes. Reasoning and task benchmarks contribute task definitions, decompositions, dependencies, intermediate steps, failures, and witnesses only when their exact training projection is rights-cleared. A project-owned reader converts either lane into the same inert episode envelope. Independent discovery strategies may rank comparable graph hypotheses, but contract and protected-transfer gates decide whether a hypothesis may proceed. Reward, frequency, a model response, and a benchmark answer have no architectural or runtime authority.

The exact plan, analysis, review, and cycle boundary

eslm-rl-dataset-discovery-plan-v2 freezes the structural question, null hypothesis, source and projection membership, baseline graph, techniques, and work policy before observation. Only the plan admits reviewed training projections. eslm-processing-graph-research-analysis-v6 records deterministic machine work with exact registry membership and an evidence ledger. eslm-processing-graph-consolidation-review-v1 is the explicit DS028/DS029 maintainer mapping of real machine hypotheses. eslm-rl-dataset-discovery-cycle-v3 binds the plan and analysis, carries that review, and accounts for every machine hypothesis as reviewed or unreviewed. No artifact creates a catalog identity. Even a complete cycle stops before implementation and promotion.

The inert research episode

eslm-research-episode-v1 is a closed analysis envelope, not an executable trajectory. It preserves enough structure to compare tasks while keeping upstream identifiers, commands, scripts, expected test answers, and environment code outside generic execution.

Episode field groupMeaning and safety boundary
Source and rightsImmutable source, component, revision, split, license, projection, and provenance identities. Episode IDs support joins and duplicate checks but never become semantic features.
Request and stateThe visible request, initial symbolic state, ordered observations, constraints, and declared capabilities. Missing state remains explicitly unobserved rather than being converted to an empty world.
Actions and dependenciesTyped action candidates, argument roles, selected kind, partial-order dependencies, and observed state deltas. Arguments are inert data; no command, import, website action, or tool call is replayed.
Outcomes and feedbackIntermediate and final outcomes, candidates, ratings, disagreement, and typed factual, procedural, safety, completeness, or style feedback. A reward is source evidence, not ESLM confidence or truth.
Truth, safety, and lossEpistemic state, truth status, privacy and safety tags, redactions, omitted components, ambiguous mappings, and every projection loss. Model rationales remain claims, never proofs.
Work receiptExact byte, row, token, graph, comparison, cluster, candidate, omission, and completeness accounting. An exhausted run retains its unvisited frontier and cannot claim a motif is absent.

Bulk clustering through several discovery methods

Bulk analysis streams projected shards into bounded structural summaries with deterministic partitions, spill and merge order, cluster membership, and incomplete-frontier accounting. Discovery techniques are intentionally plural. They vote only over comparable design candidates at a coordination node. Correlated variants share one evidence group, and a frequent pattern still has no gate authority.

Technique familyWhat it can reveal and how it is challenged
Task-frame inductionGroups ordered operations, artifact shapes, constraints, dependencies, answer domains, and capabilities. It must survive paraphrase, nonce vocabulary, changed domain, and secondary obligations.
Trajectory and phase motifsCompares typed actions, observations, retries, backtracking, state deltas, termination, and recurring transitions among interpretation, planning, evidence, execution, verification, repair, and construction.
Partial-order and graph miningFinds dependency motifs and recurring subgraphs that survive harmless action reordering. A motif fails when it relies on a source-specific tool name or fixed argument value.
Earliest-error and change pointsLocates the first divergent obligation, unavailable capability, invalid transition, rejected witness, or output-shape failure. It tests whether a proposed gate is placed before the effect it must control.
Preference and disagreementSeparates factual, procedural, safety, completeness, and style judgments instead of compressing them into one reward. Preference never substitutes for proof or source authority.
Counterfactual and metamorphic analysisChanges one constraint, precondition, argument, observation, outcome, ordering, or name and predicts which node output should change. Meaning-changing contrasts expose attractive but unsound clusters.
Cross-source recurrenceRequires the same typed responsibility to recur across independently collected and licensed sources. Another split of the same inherited dataset is not independent transfer.

What a graph hypothesis must say

HypothesisRequired engineering contract
Processing nodeA stable typed responsibility, input and output envelopes, authority, side effects, failure vocabulary, rollback, resource dimensions, and evidence that an existing node cannot own it coherently.
Coordination nodeA stable processing responsibility that preallocates finite work to alternative strategies and compares typed candidates. It must explain why the existing coordinators cannot own the comparison and gains no gate, proof, answer, or promotion authority from votes.
Authority gateThe invariant, authoritative evidence, accept/reject/gap outcomes, and exact point before an answer-authorizing or irreversible effect. It never participates in confidence voting.
StrategyAn alternative implementation of an existing selectable node, with preconditions, confidence kind, witness, abstention, failure, finite work, and correlation group.
EdgeA typed normal or exceptional transition carrying data, authority, rollback, control, or resource state. The producer and consumer must agree on one named envelope.
Protocol fieldA named datum with one producer, validated meaning, consumers, privacy, lifecycle, and an explicit meaning when absent.
Nested circuitA stable inspectable subgraph behind one named packet boundary. It cannot hide an executor, merge authority planes, or bypass the node and gate contracts inside it.

Placement follows data dependencies and authority. A proposal identifies the earliest point where its inputs exist and the latest point before the effect it controls. A source-specific rename, hidden solver, decorative wrapper, or mixture of interpretation, retrieval, proof, and presentation is rejected before implementation.

Budgets, contamination, and falsification

Research budgets independently bound source and decompressed bytes, files, rows, tokens, fields, graph nodes and edges, comparisons, clusters, motif size, hypotheses, agent packets, elapsed work, and retained cache bytes. Preallocation gives independent methods a fair opportunity. Resource exhaustion emits an incomplete frontier, never a false absence.

Grouped splits isolate source lineage, task family, template, document, website, action graph, answer structure, and inherited upstream component. Renaming, argument permutation, irrelevant evidence, safe reordering, meaning-preserving transformation, and meaning-changing contrast test whether a proposal is generic. A hypothesis is falsified when its gain disappears after identifier removal, depends on one vocabulary or source order, duplicates an existing responsibility, weakens a gate, exceeds finite work, or fails independent transfer.

Every analysis receipt records source and projection digests, rights and visibility, method identities, correlation groups, feature schema, budgets, cluster support and opposition, omissions, graph placement, contamination checks, ablation, protected-pool state, and reviewer decision. Protected rows are never copied into public receipts.

A multi-year path to broader symbolic task handling

The current foundation has built the registry, inert episode schema, feature boundary, deterministic discovery ensemble, exact analysis lineage, one complete two-source pilot chain, a sharded train-only OASST1 projection, and approved source-local and combined plans. The next acceptance gate is a current plan-v2 → analysis-v6 → human review → cycle-v3 chain for each scale scope, followed by ablation and independent protected transfer for any retained candidate. The task-structure phase compares frame, sequence, graph, change-point, earliest-error, disagreement, metamorphic, and cross-source methods. The processing-graph phase may implement a small number of non-default candidates only after those gates, then must prove their value with ablation and transfer. Later phases study long multi-obligation requests, stateful interaction, capability planning, verification, repair, and claim-shaped construction.

The target is increasingly broad task handling associated with language-model systems while retaining deterministic replay, typed inability, provenance, finite resources, and non-voting authority gates. Progress is reported per layer and per promoted capability. It is never summarized as a percentage of general intelligence.
