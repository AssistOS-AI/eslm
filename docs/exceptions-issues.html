Limits that are proved, procedural, unavailable, or still unresolved

A failed benchmark case is not automatically impossible. This page records the evidence required for each kind of limit and prevents missing knowledge, missing code, an exhausted evaluation partition, or an unavailable answer key from being mislabeled as a fundamental barrier.

Five categories that must remain distinct

CategoryRequired evidence and operational consequence
Structural non-identifiabilityTwo inputs are indistinguishable under the declared observable representation and required invariances, yet the oracle requires different outputs. The proof must exhibit the mapping and conflicting labels. The system must preserve ambiguity or acquire additional observable evidence.
Evaluation-protocol exhaustionA formerly untouched partition has been scored or inspected. It remains valid evidence for the frozen checkpoint, but cannot be used repeatedly for repair and still be described as fresh. Work may continue on development evidence; a later fresh claim needs a new sealed partition.
Missing capability or knowledgeThe task is well-defined and the oracle exists, but the parser, inference method, event model, ontology, or factual sources are incomplete. This is ordinary implementation work, not impossibility.
Unavailable local oracleThe system can produce predictions, but the delivered artifact does not contain labels or requires an external evaluator. No local accuracy may be claimed until the declared evaluation path supplies a denominator and oracle.
Unresolved diagnosisThe available traces do not yet isolate the cause. The case remains an issue with explicit competing hypotheses; it must not be presented as a proved limitation.

Proved current exception: CLUTRR structured-label non-identifiability

Let the observable input be a typed graph G containing people, family-relation edges, gender metadata, and ordered query endpoints. A general kinship method must be invariant under a bijective renaming of the people: if every node is renamed while all types, edges, genders, and query order are preserved, the inferred relation must not change.

The inspected source contains fully isomorphic projected graphs for which the official labels conflict. In one witnessed class, two renamed graphs require mother while an indistinguishable third requires mother-in-law. A corresponding conflict exists between father and father-in-law. If f is any deterministic rename-invariant method over the declared graph, graph isomorphism requires f(G₁) = f(G₂) = f(G₃). The conflicting oracle labels require at least two different values. Therefore no such method can be correct on every member of the class.

This proves only that the current projected structured evidence does not identify every official label. It does not prove that CLUTRR, kinship reasoning, or narrative relation extraction is generally impossible. A richer source representation may carry a distinction that the projection omitted. Until such observable evidence is validated, guessing from a person's name, row identifier, answer frequency, or label order would violate generalization. The current runtime returns AMBIGUOUS for the affected cases.

Proved scoring exception: IIBench syllogism conclusion strength

The published syllogism prompt asks for a categorical relationship logically entailed by two premises. Under the benchmark's traditional existential-import assumptions, A entails its I subaltern and E entails its O subaltern. Therefore a premise pair that entails A has at least two correct answers, A and I; a pair that entails E has at least E and O. The published equivalence scorer accepts reversible transformations but does not accept subalternation.

The development source contains sixteen rename-equivalence classes in which all observable structural fields agree—ordered premise forms, complement parity, figure, transformed premise position, and transformation type—while the gold forms conflict between A and I or between E and O. Across those classes, a deterministic method invariant under complete term renaming must choose the same conclusion strength, while the source key requires different strengths. No such method can match every key without reading mood, row identity, vocabulary, or another non-logical discriminator.

ESLM returns the strongest low-complement conclusion true in every finite population satisfying the premises. The development receipt verifies sound conclusions for every syllogism, while strict gold-equivalence remains lower because the stronger result is not scorer-equivalent to its subaltern. Three honest resolutions exist: accept every sound entailed categorical conclusion, make the requested mood or conclusion form part of the visible task, or retain the strict source score and count stronger sound conclusions separately. The committed proof contains aggregates and no copied answer rows.

Proved visible-query exception: bAbI Task 5 multi-transfer questions

For 128 train-visible Task-5 questions, at least two preceding transfer events have the same visible agent, recipient, event type, and requested theme role but distinct theme values. The question supplies no temporal boundary, ordinal, uniqueness condition, or visible support selector. Both themes therefore satisfy the event-role query. The generic executor returns every satisfying value with AMBIGUOUS.

The proof groups these cases into 23 alpha-renamed structural signatures. Five signatures require contradictory gold selections among structurally equivalent values, falsifying always-first, always-latest, direct-transfer priority, and pass priority. Host-only support-line identifiers explain which event the source generator intended, but using them would move oracle data into the task. A single-label contract needs an observable selector, an all-valid-values scorer, or an explicit decision that support annotations are visible input. See the full protocol explanation.

Current procedural limits

The protected EWoK fresh complement has been scored once after a frozen candidate. Its exact denominator and result belong to the live benchmark report, not to this durable issue description. The receipt remains useful checkpoint evidence, but its outcomes can no longer guide repeated repairs under a “fresh” label. The observed ties indicate missing discrimination in world knowledge. A new ontology can be developed from independent sources and development-visible controls, but another fresh claim requires a new partition that was not opened during that work.

The LogicBench evaluation source has likewise been opened once for a frozen candidate. Its aggregate exposes a substantial development-to-evaluation language generalization gap, but individual evaluation failures cannot become a repair list while that checkpoint remains labeled fresh. Further work must use the augmented development source, independently authored nonce and contrastive constructions, or a newly frozen development source. A later fresh claim needs a new untouched pool.

Story Cloze supplies labels for the validation artifact used by the current development protocol. Its delivered official test artifact is label-free and requires the external official evaluator. ESLM may generate a prediction file; it cannot truthfully display a local test accuracy without the external result. This is an unavailable local oracle, not an execution failure.

FOLIO v0.0 has no released official test file in the pinned source. Its train and validation files are development-visible, so even complete validation execution cannot be relabeled as fresh. The IIBench fresh partition has been opened exactly once for its frozen categorical candidate and is exhausted for later changes. PrOntoQA's protected partition has likewise been opened exactly once after its scalable Boolean candidate was frozen; its aggregate remains evidence for that checkpoint and cannot become an iterative repair pool. ProverQA's protected annotation-assisted partition has now been executed under an aggregate-only protocol; its few unresolved outcomes were not exposed and cannot guide later tuning. SATBench's protected partition has been opened once for the source-annotated CNF track; its result remains valid for that frozen solver, but a natural-language compiler needs different untouched evidence rather than reuse of those cases as an iterative oracle.

ZebraLogic's public release redacts all reference assignments. Its fresh public-clue result proves that 791 puzzles produced clue-satisfying unique assignments; it cannot establish official cell accuracy without the gated private oracle. The nine sealed parser failures are aggregate-only and cannot be inspected for repair under the same fresh lifecycle. Accepting the private-source terms permits a separate oracle comparison, but does not make those nine cases development data.

Current capability and knowledge gaps

BLiMP failures currently concern incomplete phrase structure, attachment, lexical selection, agreement, valency, participle interpretation, and binding domains. EWoK requires broader independently justified physical, social, material, quantitative, and spatial relations. Story Cloze requires richer goals, causal consequences, social expectations, contradiction handling, and temporal event structure. SimpleQA separates unsupported interrogative forms from missing encyclopedic facts. None of these gaps currently has an impossibility proof.

IIBench categorical semantics is implemented; its remaining strict-score difference is the proved conclusion-strength issue above, not a missing method. ProofWriter shows that the safe-Horn executor can operate on official inert logical forms while the ordinary frontend still cannot compile the corresponding general English theories. PrOntoQA's former exponential enumeration gap is closed for the frozen source by the generic scalable Boolean method. FOLIO now uses the same certificate-producing fallback when its grounded theory exceeds the small exhaustive evaluator; its remaining issues concern formula syntax, named-domain inconsistency, first-order interpretation, and natural-language compilation rather than source size or the former atom threshold. ProverQA's complete development formula track passes after provenance-bearing predicate reconciliation, but it has no direct-English claim. SATBench's Boolean search is complete for the frozen annotation track, while generated natural-language constraint compilation remains unimplemented. LogicBench exposes incomplete generalization of the controlled argument compiler. These are concrete implementation programs, not impossibility claims.

StepGame's remaining validation cases contain malformed or ambiguous official generator templates, including a pattern that omits an entity placeholder. The solver does not infer the missing endpoint from the answer. SpaRP PS1's former topology, containment, distance, and front/behind method gap is closed on the complete development validation configuration by generic declarative qualitative closure, with every returned proof replayed. The method is registered and the complete development source has also been rerun through the public engine task boundary. The official PS1 test split remains untouched.

SpaRP PS2 still contains 69 official validation targets that disagree with the relation entailed by their own relation-complete quantified symbolic context. The vector executor solves all 4,976 cases and its independent verifier replays every displacement; 4,907 outputs match the official target. For each of the other 69, changing the answer to match the key would require contradicting the accepted vector equations rather than adding a missing spatial rule. The receipt therefore classifies them as source-oracle incompatibilities and retains the derived relation. This is a source-level scoring conflict for the declared PS2 representation, not permission to branch on row identity, expected answer, relation word, or benchmark name.

ReClor and LogiQA now have complete development task construction but no argument-comprehension method. Their NO_APPLICABLE_METHOD totals are not evidence that the answers are unknowable and are not rendered as 0% accuracy. A bounded monadic categorical candidate validator was implemented as an isolated research candidate. It is sound on typed all, none, some, and some-not propositions, supports necessary-conclusion and sufficient-premise selection, and returns a conflict proof or finite countermodel for every candidate. It passes nonce renaming, option permutation, semantic reversal, resource, and witness-tamper controls.

SLR-Bench no longer has a blanket missing-method status. Finite connected positive conjunctive rules are registered and solve 126 of all 200 official validation cases with replayed evidence. Ten cases have no separator in the completely explored declared conjunctive space. Sixty-four reach an explicit candidate or match-search limit; these remain engineering and search-policy gaps and remain in the denominator. The visible 18,253 train-plus-validation rules contain no self-recursive target, so recursion cannot be learned or evaluated from that visible source. Quantification, negation, inequality, arithmetic, aggregation, and extrema require separately typed hypothesis languages and verifiers.

The method was deliberately not registered because the strict English projection found no complete visible ReClor or LogiQA task satisfying all of its preconditions. Most questions request a different operation—weakening, strengthening, flaw, role, analogy, explanation, exception, or assignment—and the remaining passages require conditionals, relations, cardinalities, order, causality, or discourse boundaries outside monadic categorical logic. Dropping opaque premises, executing only parsed candidates, collapsing compound claims to atoms, or guessing the conclusion boundary would change task meaning. The next work is therefore broader typed language and argument semantics, not promotion of an unused method and not answer-position heuristics.

Dataset size is also not an impossibility category. Large real sources may require streaming compilation, indexes, shards, bounded caches, and query-directed execution. A resource policy may stop one execution with an explicit resource-limited result, but it does not authorize silently dropping training or evaluation rows and then reporting a comprehensive denominator. Sampling remains a declared evidence choice; exhaustive execution must be reported when it is actually performed.

How an issue leaves this page

A capability gap closes when a generic or declarative improvement passes renamed, negative, and metamorphic controls and the relevant development evaluation. A procedural limitation closes only under a new valid evaluation lifecycle. An unavailable-oracle issue closes when the official or declared evaluator returns verifiable results. A structural exception closes only if additional observable evidence breaks the proved equivalence class or the task contract changes transparently.
