Open research decisions and the evidence required to make them

This page is the human-facing index of unresolved research choices. Linked DS Question/Options entries retain normative authority over contract-shaping alternatives, while source-specific and operational choices may remain here or in receipts. Each item names the evidence still needed, safe work that can proceed now, and the event that closes it; current measurements remain in the live benchmark report.

A design is selected only after generic tests and development evidence distinguish the options. Fresh or protected evaluation is not used as an iterative design oracle.

Scalable first-order entailment beyond grounded Boolean formulas

Normative options: DS015 Question #3 owns the choice among query-directed first-order proof, finite-model domain growth, or two separately declared methods. The propositional scaling question is closed: ESLM now has proof-producing Tseitin-CNF and DPLL search, while finite enumeration remains its bounded reference. Neither method supplies first-order unification, equality, or completeness over unbounded domains.

Evidence required: substitutions and variable-standardization traces, proof or countermodel witnesses, equality policy, safe relevance pruning, finite-model completeness conditions, equivalence with the Boolean methods after sound finite grounding, scaling curves on development-visible quantified theories, and nonce/reordering controls. Treating named constants as a complete domain without a source contract is not acceptable evidence.

IIBench syllogism scorer contract

Decision needed: whether research reporting should retain strict equivalence to the source-selected conclusion, add a logically-sound-conclusion metric, or expose a requested mood in an explicitly modified task. The categorical method and traditional existential-import policy are now implemented; the remaining choice concerns an underdetermined output contract, not a missing solver.

Evidence required: the committed rename-equivalence proof, confirmation from the benchmark authors about whether the hidden mood is intended as visible instruction, and a scorer comparison showing strict gold equivalence versus premise-model entailment. Row identity or lexical content cannot resolve this choice.

Natural-language theory compilation

Normative options: DS003 Question #1 owns the choice among an Earley/feature grammar, source-declared deterministic grammar adapters, or a staged combination. Logical-form success currently does not establish direct-English competence.

Evidence required: operator and scope preservation, formula alignment, direct-versus-logical-form metrics, alternate paraphrases, negation and quantifier contrasts, and no answer feedback. The Language Agent may diagnose unsupported surface forms but cannot be the benchmark reasoner.

Richer narrative and world-event representation

Normative options: DS005 Question #4 owns the choice among event-linked edges, separate state/goal/causal record classes, or continued provider-specific declarative profiles. Independently sourced evidence must calibrate defeasible ranking without turning one benchmark's labels into weights.

Evidence required: cross-source event identities, signed contradiction examples, provider-order invariance, fresh narratives, route-specific traces, and an explicit distinction among deduction, default conclusion, abduction, and ranking.

New fresh evidence after opened pools

Decision needed: which independently generated or officially unused material can serve as the next fresh regime for families whose earlier complement has already been opened. The old aggregate remains valid only for its frozen checkpoint.

Evidence required: label-blind membership, source authority, strata, membership digest, frozen behavioral dependencies, aggregate-only execution, and no post-result tuning. Reusing an opened pool under a new name is prohibited.

Document-KB pilot source

Decision needed: which legally distributable technical document is small enough for the first complete DS014 pipeline while still containing definitions, procedures, revisions, temporal scope, tables, and cross-section questions.

Evidence required: redistribution clarity, complete structural decoding, source-to-record mapping, held-out questions, proof-to-span validation, canonical/compiled equivalence, and low-memory lazy execution. Benchmark completion remains the immediate priority, so source selection may proceed without claiming the compiler exists.

Operational issues outside design decisions

A missing parser construction, missing fact, missing relation composition, unimplemented solver, or ordinary resource profile is engineering work and proceeds under its existing contract. A proved indistinguishability class belongs on Exceptions and issues. A current percentage, tested denominator, or access action belongs in the live report. A generic contract choice must link to its DS Options:; this page alone may retain only operational or source-specific choices for which several defensible actions remain.
