Knowledge Bases / space-geography

Solar system and broad geography

space-geography contains 20 entities and 35 direct facts. It has no rules, so its standalone executable count is also 35. It demonstrates selective retrieval across classifications, containment, colors, and directed spatial relations.

What can be queried

The module classifies the Sun as a star, the Moon as a natural satellite, and eight named planets as planets. It places those ten astronomical bodies in the Solar System. Mars is represented as red, while Earth and Neptune are represented as blue in the simplified visible-color vocabulary. Seven continents and two oceans are located on Earth.

Three continent-scale relations are included: Europe north of Africa, North America north of South America, and Asia north of Australia. These are intentionally coarse educational relations. They are not claims about every point, border geometry, political territory, or coordinate system.

Tested commands

node src/cli.mjs ask "Is Mars a planet?" --kb space-geography
node src/cli.mjs ask "Where is Neptune?" --kb space-geography
node src/cli.mjs ask "What color is Mars?" --kb space-geography
node src/cli.mjs ask "What is north of Africa?" --kb space-geography

These produce, respectively, Yes., Neptune is in Solar System., Mars is red., and Europe is north of Africa.. Questions about countries, capitals, distances, orbital order, moons other than Earth's Moon, or current geographical facts return UNKNOWN or UNSUPPORTED because this module does not encode them.
