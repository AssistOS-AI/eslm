Knowledge Bases / animals

Animals and ordinary capabilities

animals contains 12 species entities, 21 direct facts, 5 rules, and 45 facts after standalone closure. It separates taxonomy from capability evidence so familiar exceptions remain correct under the current monotonic reasoner.

Taxonomy and capabilities

Sparrow, robin, eagle, penguin, and ostrich are classified as birds. Whale, dolphin, dog, cat, and cow are mammals; frog is an amphibian and salmon is a fish. Rules map each of those four classes to animal and animal to living-being. Flight is asserted only for sparrow, robin, and eagle. Swimming is asserted for penguin, whale, dolphin, and salmon; ostrich can run and frog can jump.

The module cannot express explicit negation, defaults with exceptions, habitat, diet, anatomy, or biological uncertainty. UNKNOWN for penguin flight means the active KB has no positive proof; it is not yet the stronger claim “penguins cannot fly”. A later non-monotonic or explicit-negative layer must represent that distinction directly.

Tested commands

node src/cli.mjs ask "Can Sparrow fly?" --kb animals
node src/cli.mjs ask "Is Penguin an animal?" --kb animals
node src/cli.mjs ask "Can Penguin swim?" --kb animals
node src/cli.mjs ask "Can Penguin fly?" --kb animals

The second query is a one-step deduction. The first and third are direct facts. The fourth returns UNKNOWN. Loading --kb all allows the derived living-being fact to activate growth and mortality rules from child-basic; provenance then crosses two named modules.
