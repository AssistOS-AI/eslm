Long conversational benchmark

This is ESLM's generated internal stress and regression suite, not a public benchmark and not evidence of comparison with another model. It checks longer contexts, surface variation, loaded WordNet and ATOMIC queries, explicit uncertainty, and unsupported requests.

1000 / 1000accepted cases

Generator seed conversation-stress-v2 makes the accepted run reproducible. All 1000 inputs are unique. Operand masking identifies 536 distinct linguistic structures, and no one structure occurs more than 10 times. These checks prevent a large score made mostly from renamed copies of one sentence.

The run used lazy public-KB shards with 64 MiB per provider. Queries took 41.39 seconds after 0.00 seconds of initialization. RSS changed by 273.1 MiB during the process.
