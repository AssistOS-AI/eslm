ESLM Documentation / Evaluation and claims

What each result actually proves

ESLM has real symbolic mechanisms and substantial benchmark-adapter work, but the evidence is heterogeneous. A parser score, a structured-task run, a formula solver, an authored fixture, and an official end-to-end test are not interchangeable. This page makes the route, denominator, split, receipt, and remaining gap visible.

The default internal benchmark has two reports

npm run benchmark first executes the five-case authored fixture and then the deterministic generated heuristic suite. The command deliberately preserves two result artifacts and two denominators. latest-benchmark.html keeps the small cases readable. latest-generated-heuristic-benchmark.html summarizes the broad development run; its JSON receipt is the machine authority.

The generated suite defaults to 1,200 cases, 43 technique templates, 18 domain themes, 28 target families, and the stable seed eslm-generated-heuristic-development-v1. The generator repeatedly instantiates those 43 shapes with nonce content and domain combinations; the row count measures repeated execution breadth, not 1,200 independent structures. It derives a typed oracle from template variables before invoking the real local heuristic runtime. Language Agent assistance and failure grounding are disabled, so the measurement follows direct parsing, local proposal families, safety checks, voting, reparse, and the final symbolic status without external repair or unrelated retrieval.

The 18 current domain records contain 15 distinct base predicate lemmas, and each record fixes its predicate. A cluster labeled acoustics, communication, or rigging can therefore be driven by the morphology of buzz, pass, or tie, not by domain semantics. Domain labels help organize this distribution, but they are not an independent domain-by-verb generalization test. The realism plan crosses themes and morphology classes independently and reserves unseen lemmas.

At most 24 representative failures are printed for review, while every generated case remains in the fixed denominator and aggregate clusters. Research starts from the largest repeated cluster: state a generic structural hypothesis, add renamed and meaning-changing controls, change a strategy only at its typed boundary, and replay both the fixed seed and another seed. This workflow turns many executions into cumulative evidence and prevents the memorable wording of one example from becoming a runtime branch.

A pass has the authority of its oracle level. Entailment cases may require answer execution; interpreted questions require Semantic IR; decompositions require query-local retained structure; requests require executed plans and output contracts; negative controls require safe abstention; and proposal-only cases require a retained candidate with protected operators. A proposal-only row may pass while the final status remains UNPARSED. That is useful proposal-generation and safety evidence, not executable interpretation coverage. The report aggregates these six levels separately. Its machine field named accuracy is still exactly passed / total across all levels, so the headline remains a mixed development-contract rate and must be read with oracle-level, route, and status tables.

Semantic success and heuristic activation are scored separately. A direct parse that reaches the oracle's meaning is a successful end-to-end result, not a failure caused by skipping an unnecessary repair. It is recorded as a bypass for the intended family. A template claimed to test recovery must first demonstrate direct UNPARSED or direct Semantic IR that differs from the structural oracle, whether the direct status is UNKNOWN, SOLVED, or PARTIAL. This distinction keeps the suite from penalizing broader direct-language support while still exposing permissive parses that need a licensed local repair.

The current generator covers deletion, insertion, substitution, and transposition spelling processes plus explicit silent-e, doubled-consonant, sibilant, final-y, ie-ending, and terminal-z morphology strata. The next realism layer adds template-disjoint, vocabulary-disjoint, and independently authored packets; publishes distinct template, predicate, morphology-class, and technique-by-domain counts; crosses domains and morphology independently; balances underrepresented cells; and adds multi-edit, irregular, and genuinely ambiguous nonce forms. A difficult mutation remains in the evidence even when it lowers the rate. When several repairs are plausible, the system should retain calibrated alternatives or return AMBIGUOUS, or consult an explicitly reviewed lexical resource; the benchmark must not reward a hard-coded verb branch.

Read the label before the number. Historical-stale evidence remains useful as a checkpoint, but it is not a claim about the current worktree. “Executed” means a declared task ran; it does not mean the raw benchmark prose passed through the ordinary language frontend.

How to read one row

  • Track identifies what is measured: raw-language, structured-task, or solver-conformance.
  • Input route identifies what entered the system: raw language, a source template, a structured task, or a source annotation. A formula, graph, CNF, finite program, or host-projected task can test a real generic solver without testing prose understanding.
  • Evidence state and split quality distinguish development, diagnostic, one-shot fresh, grouped structural holdout, row-IID holdout, and official test. “Fresh” describes lifecycle, not necessarily difficult generalization.
  • Checkpoint state says whether a stored execution is current, stale, unrecoverable, unverified, invalid, or unavailable. historical-unverified means an execution receipt exists but no complete currentness audit covers that receipt family. Execution time, source/partition/oracle/scorer identity, selected methods and KB versions, resource policy, measured time and memory, replay command, and content-addressed behavior identity are required before a row can support a current release claim.
  • Diagnosis comes from the generated report. HTML pages do not keep a second mutable copy of counts or explanations.

selectedKbVersions means packages available to routing, consultedKbVersions means providers or indexes actually queried, and usedKbVersions means evidence that contributed to the supported answer or verified task result. Related grounding evidence has its own contributor versions and never enters answer provenance.

Denominators and abstention

For forced-choice tasks, every eligible case remains in the end-to-end denominator. Reports carry three complementary metrics:

The generated row's forcedChoice value is joined from the typed benchmark contract and validated; it is not trusted as free-form receipt metadata.

  • end-to-end accuracy = correct / all eligible cases;
  • attempt coverage = in-domain predictions / all eligible cases;
  • selective accuracy = correct / attempted predictions, or null when there were no attempts.

Therefore a system that predicts nothing on a non-empty forced-choice set has 0% end-to-end accuracy, 0% attempt coverage, and no selective accuracy. By contrast, a task whose private correctness oracle is unavailable has no accuracy denominator; it must report a separately named completion, consistency, witness, or uniqueness metric.

Why several high scores are narrower than they look

The implementation genuinely contains Horn deduction, categorical reasoning, finite-model search, DPLL, relation closure, spatial composition, state transitions, and finite constraint solving. Several reported tracks feed those mechanisms source formulas, annotations, structured graphs, or task-local projections. That is valid solver or adapter evidence. It is not raw-language evidence, and the dashboard must keep an independent language diagnostic visible where one exists.

The strongest architecture evidence is structural: generic runtime directories contain no benchmark-family or row-ID dispatch, and the test suite includes nonce renaming, negative controls, changed ordering, proof replay, countermodel validation, and witness checks. The next high-value gates strengthen claim provenance and language generalization while preserving those solver controls.

More realistic generalization

Hashing individual rows into development and “fresh” pools is often only an in-distribution holdout. Future partitions group the causal unit that could leak: generator world or seed, story, paraphrase template, lexicon and predicate vocabulary, relation composition, proof graph and depth, spatial path, or source document. Correlated decisions from one source item remain in one group.

Each report uses an explicit split-quality label such as row-IID, grouped-world, grouped-template, vocabulary-disjoint, structure-disjoint, source-version, or official-test. Opened fresh pools remain historical checkpoints; later repairs require a genuinely new pool rather than relabeling the old cases.

Grounded-failure product benchmark

The new fallback needs its own independently authored, frozen evaluation. Cases include answerable, partially answerable, unanswerable, ambiguous, conflicting, malformed, typo and paraphrase, multilingual, multi-KB, and wrong-KB-distractor questions. Expected answer support and acceptable related records stay host-only.

Release sequence

  1. Truth gate: enforce row schema, denominator semantics, track and route labels, strict prediction imports, and receipt dependency audits.
  2. Reproducible baseline: freeze a clean checkpoint and rerun feasible rows in isolated processes with measured time and peak memory.
  3. Structural splits: build grouped and disjoint development pools; keep raw language separate from annotations and task adapters.
  4. Grounding evaluation: freeze the unanswered-question suite and score primary answers, retrieval, provenance, and optional formulation separately.
  5. External validation: export label-free predictions, use official evaluators where permitted, and compare symbolic, no-retrieval, retrieval, and external-model tracks under the same evidence scope.
